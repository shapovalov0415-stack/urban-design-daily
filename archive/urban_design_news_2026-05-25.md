## 33 Global Cities Commit to Climate-Responsive Urban Planning Model by 2035

**Source:** Environment+Energy Leader  
**URL:** https://environmentenergyleader.com/stories/thirty-three-cities-commit-to-climate-urban-planning-model-by-2035,126896  
**Topics:** Climate Adaptation, Urban Planning, International, Policy, Densification

At the World Urban Forum in Baku, Azerbaijan, on May 20, 2026, C40 Cities and UN-Habitat formally launched the Urban Planning Accelerator with 33 signatory cities spanning six continents—including Chicago, Paris, Tokyo, Jakarta, Karachi, and Johannesburg—pledging to adopt a climate-responsive urban planning model by 2035. The initiative, originally unveiled at the C40 World Mayors Summit in Rio de Janeiro in November 2025, commits cities to prioritize densification over urban sprawl, restrict new development in high climate-risk zones, protect and restore natural systems that buffer against extreme weather, and embed climate considerations into planning processes. The accelerator addresses urgent trends: urban land area is expanding up to 50 percent faster than population growth globally, and if current trajectories continue, urban areas could triple in size by 2050, while green spaces in and around cities have shrunk by nearly 29 percent between 1990 and 2020. The Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change projects that cities adopting compact, mixed-use, transit-oriented planning models could cut greenhouse gas emissions by up to 26 percent by 2050. More than one billion people currently live in slums or informal settlements, underscoring the social equity dimensions of climate-informed planning. The accelerator is designed to close the gap between climate mitigation potential and current development trajectories.

**Why it matters:** This coordinated international commitment establishes a benchmark for climate-responsive planning policy, embedding densification, climate-risk mapping, and natural system protection as core urban design principles that could reshape global development trajectories over the next decade.
